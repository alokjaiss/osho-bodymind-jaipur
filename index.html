Why the Mystic Rose

Somewhere along the way, we learned to hold it in.

  • When did you last laugh until your stomach hurt, for no reason at all?
  • How many tears have you swallowed because it wasn't the right time, or the right place?
  • And when you finally sit to meditate, why won't the mind go quiet?

Osho saw that most of us carry two layers between ourselves and silence: the laughter we never let out, and beneath it, the tears we never allowed. The Mystic Rose works through both, one week at a time, so that silence isn't something you have to force. It's what is left.

“I have invented many meditations, but perhaps this will be the most essential and fundamental one.”

Osho, on the Mystic Rose

The process

Three weeks. One rose, opening from the outside in.

Laughter is the outer petal. Tears lie beneath it. Silence is the heart.

Week one · Laughter

Week one

Laughter हँसी

3 hours a day · 7 days

Every day for three hours, the group laughs, for no reason at all. It starts awkward, maybe a little forced. Then someone's laughter catches yours and it stops being an exercise. Years of seriousness begin to loosen.

Week two

Tears आँसू

3 hours a day · 7 days

Then, three hours a day, you let the tears come. No story is needed and nobody asks why. Whatever was swallowed, whether grief, frustration or tenderness, gets room to move, in a held space where everyone is doing the same.

Week three

Silence मौन

The Watcher on the Hill · 3 hours a day · 7 days

With the laughter and tears let out, you simply sit and watch. Silence here isn't an effort. It's what remains. OSHO Dynamic Meditation each morning is part of this final week.

Questions, answered.

I've never meditated. Is this too much for a beginner?

No. Beginners are guided step by step, and there's an orientation on arrival day. You don't need any technique. Laughing, crying and sitting are things everyone already knows how to do.

What if I can't laugh, or can't cry?

Almost everyone wonders this. Nobody is asked to perform. You keep showing up for the three hours and let whatever happens, happen. Often it begins as pretending and turns real. Some days it won't, and that is part of the process too.

Is this like laughter yoga or a laughter club?

No. There are no exercises or breathing techniques. Laughter is the first week of a 21-day process that moves through tears into silent meditation.

Can I join for only one or two weeks?

The three weeks are designed as one journey, and each builds on the last. If the full 21 days isn't possible for you, message us before booking and we'll talk it through.

What does it cost?

Your contribution depends on the room you choose: AC Deluxe, AC Dormitory or Non-AC Dormitory. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send the current contribution. We accept UPI, bank transfer, or cash at reception.

What language is it in?

Instructions and talks are in English and Hindi.

How do I get to the centre?

Search “Osho Meditation Centre, Jaipur” on Google Maps. It's about 45–75 minutes from the city depending on traffic, with parking on site. Message us and we'll help you plan the trip from the railway station or airport.
